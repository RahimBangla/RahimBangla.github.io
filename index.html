About

Who I am, where I studied and what I'm working on right now.

  • My story

    My first step was a small one. In 2011 I opened a Gmail account and made a Facebook profile, and I couldn't stop wondering how it all worked behind the screen. So in 2012 I started learning XHTML and put together my first website. I hosted it on Wapka, a free site builder where you could make and publish a mobile site right from your phone. It wasn't much, but seeing something I made live on the internet got me hooked. In 2013 I learned my first programming language, C, and in 2015 I went to Chittagong for my first national programming contest. That's where my journey in tech really began.

    In 2016 came my first big achievement: I became a founding member and the network engineer of Roshni Communications, a new internet service provider startup in Cumilla, designing and running its network and operations center as the business grew. Then I moved up the stack, one step at a time: mobile apps for the logistics sector at BMS-Tech-Logistics, websites and IT for the nonprofit Youth for Bangladesh, and technical support for Market24.com at Enter Idea.

    In 2022, a few months into my CSE degree at Green University of Bangladesh, I joined HomePay as a QA engineer. I worked my way up through software engineer and senior programmer, and today I'm the technical lead in charge of the Mobile Wallet Remittance Aggregator, moving money from around the world into MFS wallets in Bangladesh.

    Outside work I love traveling and exploring new places around the world, so if you know somewhere worth seeing, say hi.
